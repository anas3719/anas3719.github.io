# Work Card Assets

Release updated at the owner's request on 2026-11-08. 93 of 101 work cards have sourced
or owner-supplied logo files. The owner approved the remaining 8 cards as
name-only entries. Work order, links, videos and categories are unchanged.

## Originals And Fidelity

The catalog uses original bytes, without AI redrawing, recoloring, tracing or
upscaling. AI restoration experiments were excluded and archived outside this
repository after the owner's clarification. Known AI-named files in the supplied
archive were not imported. Owner-supplied files are recorded separately from
assets downloaded directly from an official website. They are not all independently
verified against a current official website.

`sources.json` records source pages, asset URLs or supplied filenames, native
dimensions and SHA-256 digests. Source provenance and brand matching are separate
from browser rendering tests. Brand marks remain their owners' property.
Card background choices are presentation, not certified brand guidelines.

Shurfa was matched to the tea store, not the similarly named property developer.
The owner confirmed zaad.org.sa and approved its current logo on 2026-11-03;
Zad now uses the unchanged original downloaded from that website. Uncertain work names deliberately
remain text, rather than receiving a plausible but unrelated logo.

The latest 13 additions use first-party website, broadcaster, store or linked
official social-profile assets. Mokab was matched to the exact `mokab_rs` handle
visible in the ad; Ingame was matched to the advertised app interface. COZ uses
the larger original from its official profile's linked page. Partner-academy,
production-agency and unrelated namesake logos were rejected. Some social-profile
originals remain small; fidelity takes priority over invented detail.

The owner supplied sheikhaaloud.com, amoudiholidays.com and the 95celsiuscafe
Instagram profile on 2026-11-08. Sheikha and Amoudi now use their original website
header logos. The 596px Celsius original was retained after checking its linked
official profile; the catalog now matches the owner's renamed `95 Celsius` work.

The eight name-only cards are Watan Bila Mukhalif campaign, Nqaa water, Manara,
From Zero to Publishing, Eyes on the Sea, the Eyes on the Sea event, and the
newly added Ford and Mohl works. No sufficiently reliable original logo was
imported for these entries.
This is not a claim that their logos do not exist online. Specific uncertainty,
checked dates and the owner's name-only approval are recorded in `sources.json`.

## Presentation

Logos occupy the main card area and keep their original proportions. Optional
`frame` coordinates hide measured empty outer margins in the UI only. Original
files and their colors remain unchanged, and the complete logo remains visible.
The category badge was removed from each card because the category tabs already
identify the section. A brand label and play/folder icon remain below the artwork.

`work-brand-assets.js` is separate from admin-editable work data, so a normal
admin save cannot overwrite these files. New or unknown work names fall back to
text. A failed image also falls back to its work name.

## Verification

Automated browser checks passed at 1440, 390 and 320 CSS pixels for both
categories: 54 regular works, 47 UGC works, logo decoding, complete logo bounds,
Arabic label fit, no horizontal overflow, modal opening/closing and keyboard focus.
The original folder link and missing-image fallback were separately tested.
These checks do not prove all Google Drive videos play or every brand identity.

Some authentic originals are below 2x display density, especially larger desktop
cards. They were not generatively replaced or described as high-resolution.
Browser density diagnostics are retained outside the repository.

Icons: Lucide `circle-play` and `folder-open`, lucide-static 0.468.0 (ISC).
Peugeot: Simple Icons 14.0.0 collection (see included license and source record).
Other vector sources and attributions are recorded in `sources.json`.
